HD Photography has been refreshed with a new design and a more personal direction.

The goal of **HD Photography V2.1** is simple: keep the photographs at the center while making the site easier and more enjoyable to explore.

## A More Personal Photography Site

HD Photography is now presented as my personal photography space — a place to share photographs from nature, travel, landscapes, and everyday life.

The site also provides a place to introduce selected **guest photographers** whose work and perspectives I admire.

## A New HĐ Photography Identity

The familiar HD Photography logo has also been updated with a small personal touch.

The letter **D** has become the Vietnamese letter **Đ**, creating the new **HĐ Photography** mark.

**HĐ** quietly represents **Hồng Đức**, while still keeping the familiar identity of HD Photography.

## Photography Showcases

The Showcase section has been redesigned so collections are no longer limited to months or years.

A showcase can now have its own descriptive name and date, making it easier to present photographs as complete visual stories.

New showcases are displayed automatically with the most recent collection first.

## Guest Photographers

Guest photographer pages have also been simplified.

The Guest Photographers gallery now focuses on the photographer's portrait and name, inviting visitors to open the individual profile to learn more about the photographer, their specialties, and their work.

## English & Vietnamese

HD Photography continues to support both **English and Vietnamese**, with navigation and major site sections available in either language.

This update is another step in making HD Photography a simple, personal place to share photographs, journeys, and the stories behind them.
