HD Photography is pleased to introduce **Bích Liên Phan** as a featured guest photographer.

Her photographs bring a gentle and thoughtful perspective to flowers, landscapes, nature, travel, and the quiet beauty found in everyday life.

## A Personal Way of Seeing

Photography is more than recording a place or subject. Each photographer brings a different way of seeing — shaped by experience, emotion, light, color, and the moments that attract their attention.

Bích Liên's work reflects that personal approach through carefully composed images and a strong appreciation for natural beauty.

## Flowers and Nature

Flowers are an important part of her photography.

Through color, form, light, and detail, familiar subjects become opportunities to explore subtle changes in mood and atmosphere.

Her nature photography extends that same sensitivity to landscapes and the environment around her.

## Landscape and Travel

Travel provides new places, changing light, and unexpected moments to photograph.

From broader landscapes to smaller details discovered along the way, these images preserve both the character of a place and the photographer's personal experience of being there.
